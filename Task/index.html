Understanding Real-Life Bullying

👊

Physical Bullying

Learn to recognize and stop behaviors like hitting, kicking, tripping, and damaging property.

🗣️

Verbal Bullying

Understand the impact of name-calling, insults, teasing, and intimidating spoken words.

🤝

Social Exclusion

Identify relational bullying, such as spreading rumors, public embarrassment, and leaving others out intentionally.
